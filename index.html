What is the dress code policy?

We maintain an upscale Smart Casual dress code. Collared shirts, tailored trousers, chic evening wear, and dress shoes are recommended. Athletic wear, baseball caps, and flip-flops are strictly prohibited.

Do you accommodate dietary restrictions?

Absolutely. Our culinary team offers bespoke gluten-free, vegan, and vegetarian offerings. Please notify your server or indicate your preferences when booking your reservation.
